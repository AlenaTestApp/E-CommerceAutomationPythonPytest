### Automated UI Scenarios (Core)

**Authentication**
- Login success shows user greeting  
- Login failure shows error message  
- Logout clears session  

**Products**
- Products list is displayed  
- Search returns matching products  
- Open product detail and verify name/price  

**Cart (Table Validation)**
- Add product to cart creates cart row  
- Update quantity recalculates total  
- Remove item deletes row  
- Cart total equals sum of row totals  

### Out of Scope
